# Portfolio Strategy

Canonical raw source: `research/jack-portfolio-scrollytelling-research-plan-updated.md`.

## Objective

Redesign the portfolio from a long CV-style page into a restrained scientific-editorial
scrollytelling site. The site should make the through-line obvious:

science, markets and software are not separate identities; they are the domains where
Jack builds decision systems.

## Audience

Primary:

- Pharma tech and R&D software recruiters.
- AI engineering, product and automation teams.
- Fintech and market-intelligence roles where software plus finance matters.

Secondary:

- IE/MCSBT peers and faculty.
- Hackathon, open-source and research collaborators.
- General technical reviewers checking evidence quality.

## Content hierarchy

Flagship chapters should be limited to four or five projects:

1. SEIL, as the strongest pharma-tech narrative, handled NDA-safely.
2. Sentinel, as geospatial crisis intelligence and hackathon proof.
3. SMILES-IUPAC, as computational chemistry and DeepChem evidence.
4. Briefly, as market intelligence and portfolio-aware automation.
5. EEG/Cognix, only if the neuro signal pipeline is ready enough to be honest.

Remaining projects belong in a dense archive with status labels, not full cinematic
chapters.

## Domain structure

Use domains, not only technologies:

- Biology and computational science.
- Neurotechnology and biosignals.
- Markets and quantitative systems.
- AI and software infrastructure.

## Rules

- Evidence beats aesthetic novelty.
- A generated asset is atmosphere, not proof of implementation.
- NDA projects need safe descriptions and safe screenshots only.
- The mobile and reduced-motion versions must preserve the story.
- Do not turn the portfolio into separate mini-sites for each domain.
